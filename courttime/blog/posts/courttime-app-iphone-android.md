CourtTime now has its own app on the **App Store** and **Google Play**. If your club or HOA runs on CourtTime, you can book a court, cancel, pay guest fees and sign up for clinics in a few taps, without opening a browser.

Here is what the app does, how to get it, and what changes for club admins.

## What members can do in the app

- **Book a court.** Open the calendar, see which courts are open, tap a time and confirm. Start and end times, booking type and notes are all on one screen.
- **Cancel just as fast.** Plans change. Cancel from your reservations in a couple of taps, and the court opens back up for someone else.
- **See your reservations.** Every upcoming booking, tennis or pickleball, in one list.
- **Add bookings to your calendar.** After you book, the app offers to add it to your phone's calendar.
- **Pay guest fees in the app.** Bringing a guest? Pay their fee before you walk on court instead of tracking down the pro shop.
- **Sign up for clinics and lessons.** See what's coming up, how many spots are left, and sign up from your phone.
- **Find a hitting partner.** Post what you're looking for, or answer someone else's post.
- **Message other members.** Set up doubles without swapping phone numbers.
- **Keep up with your club.** Announcements and events from the club's bulletin board, plus push notifications for what matters to you.

## How to get it

**If you're already a member at a CourtTime club:**

1. Search **"CourtTime"** in the App Store or Google Play, or go to [courttimeapp.com/get-app](https://courttimeapp.com/get-app).
2. Install it and log in with the account you already use on the website.
3. That's it. Your club, your reservations and your household are already there.

**If you're new to your club's CourtTime:**

1. Create an account in the app or at [courttimeapp.com](https://courttimeapp.com).
2. Search for your club and request to join.
3. Once your club approves you, you can start booking.

Nothing changes on the website. You can keep booking there, use the app, or both. Everything stays in sync.

## What it means for club admins

Your booking rules come with the app. Booking windows, reservation limits, prime-time caps, household limits and no-show strikes all apply in the app exactly as they do on the web, so members can't book around them.

You don't need to set anything up for members to start using it. If you want to tell your members, the short version is: *search "CourtTime" in your app store and log in with the account you already have.*

## Not on CourtTime yet?

CourtTime is court booking for tennis, pickleball and padel clubs and HOAs. It's **$50 per court per year**, with a $200 minimum and a $550 maximum, and it includes unlimited members, booking rules that enforce themselves, household accounts and the app.

If your club is still on an older booking system, we'd be glad to show you around. [Take a look at courttimeapp.com](https://courttimeapp.com), or read our guide to [setting fair court booking rules](/blog/fair-court-booking-rules/).
